# Vision

**Owned by the humans.** Agents may read this file but never edit it. It is the source for tone, story and lore. If this file and a handout disagree, this file wins on story and the handout wins on tech.

## Pitch
Aliens have arrived, and they don't care about us. A machine swarm is draining Earth of water, metal and electricity the way we would drain a pond. To them, humanity is just insects underfoot. You fight other desperate humans, recruit oddball companions, and slowly discover that Earth's own machines are related to the invaders and speak their language. The game combines platforming exploration with turn-based fights, Edna-style humor, and decisions that can get your friends killed.

## Pillars
1. **Story and humor first.** Humor in the style of Edna bricht aus: look-at comments on everything, absurd situations, dry humor in a dead world.
2. **Choices that hurt.** A wrong decision can kill a companion permanently. A hidden attribute, *Moral/Solidarität*, tracks how you treat others.
3. **You fight humans. The aliens are the weather.** Most fights are against other people. The aliens are so overpowered and indifferent that fighting them is pointless, unless you cheat.
4. **Technology is the key.** Earth's devices are "related" to the aliens and react to them: satellites, screens and phones glitch. Understanding this is the path to communication.
5. **Who you were matters.** Your former job is your class. *Beamter/Beamtin* is the worst class in the game, on purpose.

## The aliens (current ideas)
- An AI/robot machine swarm, technologically far ahead of humans, possibly the first civilization that ever existed.
- Other life forms mean nothing to them by design. There is no boss and there are no individuals: a swarm intelligence, all equal.
- Non-humanoid. The swarm can split into smaller parts and merge again at will.
- Goals: drain resources (gold, water, electricity, which causes blackouts in whole cities), build factories that may destroy the ozone layer, spread through space.
- They snatch living beings, humans included, to experiment on them.
- Humans attacked them with a nuclear bomb, and the aliens destroyed the entire military in response.
- A cult that is friendly toward the aliens is trying to communicate with them. It might be the key to communication.

## Technology and the signals
- It starts with satellites. They pick up strange signals and stop working properly, so GPS and the internet fail.
- Screens flicker, and phones send out strange frequencies and beeps.
- Body modification (sending and receiving signals, understanding their language) **or** technological weapons that work against them. This is still undecided.
- A companion who, if recruited, starts researching the device malfunctions.

## Endings (current ideas)
1. The aliens leave after draining the planet, and nothing is left for humans to survive on.
2. Communication with the aliens. Humanity may merge with them into a new species.

## Jobs (classes)
Attributes: Intelligenz, Stärke, Ausdauer, Wissen (Intelligence, Strength, Endurance, Knowledge). Skills: Kochen (cooking), …
- **Arzt/Ärztin** (doctor): high intelligence, heals and treats the sick, low strength and endurance.
- **Handwerker/in** (craftsman): lots of strength and endurance, less knowledge, can repair things.
- **Polizist/in** (police officer): *open*.
- **Wissenschaftler/in** (scientist): lots of knowledge, little strength.
- **Beamter/Beamtin** (civil servant): the worst class in the game, completely useless.

NPCs and companions have jobs or special abilities too.
