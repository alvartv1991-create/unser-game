# H3 — Dialogue, writer pipeline, alpha story, characters — Claude

Branch `claude/h3-dialogue-story`, started from `claude/h1-h2-cleanup` (main + the visual-merge review in `merge-2026-10-15.md`).

## Done
- **Dialogue box** `scenes/dialogue/balloon.tscn` (`scripts/dialogue/balloon.gd`): speaker name, 64×64 portrait (`[#mood=happy]` picks `<speaker>_<mood>.png`, falls back to the neutral one, then to a colored square from `speakers.json`), typewriter text skippable with E/Enter/click, choices with keyboard, gamepad and mouse. Many choices scroll in a capped panel. Language switches mid-line re-translate the shown line (fixes H0's `DialogueLine.refresh()` crash by not using it).
- **Choice conditions** (`scripts/dialogue/choice_rules.gd`): unmet job/item/companion conditions are hidden, or greyed out with the reason when the choice has `[#show_locked]` (e.g. "[Arzt/Ärztin] …", reason found from the condition, localized). Moral conditions are **always hidden**.
- **Two languages:** every line has a static id (`[ID:COOK_FIRST_MEET_03]`), English in `localization/dialogue.csv`. `TranslationSync` adds missing ids, keeps existing English, marks new lines `[EN?] <German>`, and reports German edits whose English needs re-checking. One click: dialogue tester button, the editor script `scripts/dialogue/sync_translations_editor.gd` (File → Run), or `--script res://scripts/dialogue/sync_translations.gd`. **German is read straight from the `.dialogue` files at runtime**, so German edits never wait for the sync; ids missing in English show `[EN?] <German>`, never a raw id.
- **Writer docs:** `docs/WRITING_GUIDE.md` (file format, conditions, every writer command with an example, fights, deaths, items, moral, translation step by step, testing, naming, the colon trap) and `docs/MORAL_LOG.md` (every moral change and every gated option, plus proof that both bands are reachable before the camp).
- **Dialogue tester** `scenes/dialogue/test_dialogue.tscn` (F1): file, cue, job, companions recruited/dead, items, flags, moral presets, DE/EN, play / keep state and play on / translation sync; shows moral, flags, party, items, objective; remembers the last setup.
- **Area 1 story, all `[TEMP]`, German + English (225 lines):** every file and cue from ALPHA_SCOPE § Content ids plus all 9 examine cues.
  - The cook and the tinkerer each have a first meeting with a recruit choice (can be revisited through `idle`), 3 idle lines, a glitch and a sword-breaking reaction (barks), and react to the other's death.
  - Every job has unique options; the civil servant's are always useless and funny.
  - **Moral:** 16 moral-changing choices. High (≥25) and low (≤−25) are both reachable before the camp (tested). Hidden high-moral camp pass and hidden low-moral killing of the surrendering raider (`raider_spared`).
  - **Dilemma** (`alien_zone:dilemma`): up to 6 options depending on party and job. "Cook, bang the pan" sounds reasonable and kills the cook (`story:dilemma`), walking away costs 15 moral, the tinkerer or a scientist can save the man without losses. Sets `dilemma_choice`.
  - **Deaths are remembered** through `GameState.is_alive()` (story and combat deaths): tinkerer `first_meet`/`idle`, cook `idle`, `gate:open`, plus a bark from the survivor.
  - **Cult teaser:** cryptic, no answers, sets `cult_teaser_found`.
  - Objectives at every beat (`localization/story.csv`).
- **StoryDirector** autoload (`scripts/story/story_director.gd`): after a won camp fight it starts `raiders:camp_after_fight`, after the swarm `alien_zone:after_cheat`; talking out of a fight resolves the camp/street. Barks via `EventBus.bark_requested` from `data/barks.json` (glitch with cooldown, the tinkerer reacts most; after fights; entering an area; a companion died; the holy sword broke).
- **Character art:** mood portraits `player/cook/tinkerer` × `happy/sad` (generated with the others by `tools/build_character_visuals.gd`). Sprites for all seven characters and their portraits came with the merge.
- Also answered H2's requests: H0's `hello.dialogue` is fully translated, the missing-text fallback is translated (`DLG_MISSING_TEXT`), examine cues `tv_flicker`, `payphone`, `arcade_cabinet` exist.

## How to try it
1. F5 → F1 → `dialogue/test_dialogue.tscn`. Pick e.g. `area1/raiders.dialogue` + `camp`, job `Polizist/in`, Moral "hoch" → ▶ Abspielen. You see the hidden "Erinnert ihr euch an mich?" option and the greyed-out options of other jobs. Switch DE/EN during a line.
2. Dilemma: `area1/alien_zone.dialogue` + `dilemma`, tick both companions → choose the pan. Then `area1/gate.dialogue` + `open` with **Weiterspielen** to see the death remembered.
3. Translation: change a German line, press **Übersetzungen syncen**, look at `localization/dialogue.csv`.
4. Tests: 86 passing (`tests/dialogue/test_area1_dialogue.gd`, `tests/story/test_story_director.gd` are new).

## Contract changes (the other agent must know)
All additions; nothing renamed or removed.

- Dialogue tags: `[#mood=<mood>]` on lines, `[#show_locked]` / `[#show_locked=KEY]` on choices.
- `data/barks.json` situations: `glitch`, `after_fight`, `enter_area`, `sword_broke`, `died_<companion>`.
- `data/speakers.json` gained `clerk`, `cultist`, `raider_scavenger`, `raider_brute`; `player` is always the protagonist.
