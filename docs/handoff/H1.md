# H1 — Turn-based combat, jobs, party, first characters — Claude

## Done
- **Combat logic** (`scripts/combat/`, no nodes):
  - `CombatState` runs a fight and produces events.
  - `CombatSetup` builds a fight from GameState and writes the result back **only when it's over**, so "try again" needs no snapshot.
  - `CombatRules` holds the formulas, `EnemyAI` the enemy behavior (scavenger / brute / swarm), `CheatListener` the reusable cheat codes, and `CombatText` turns events into log lines from `data/combat_text.json`.
  - `CombatAutoPilot` plays the party's side, for tests and for H5's walkthrough.
- **Rules as specified:**
  - Initiative, defending, the holy sword (breaks after exactly one hit, then switches to the best remaining weapon), cooldowns, barricade, arrest, analyze, the useless `file_form`.
  - **Downed → death:** a visible "Am Boden — n" countdown; a hit while down kills a companion; the protagonist never dies; `rules.companion_combat_death = false` makes it a plain KO.
  - **Talking:** six approaches in the new `data/talk.json`, shown with their chance in %; the Beamter's ceasefire form always fails, with its own line.
  - **Fleeing:** never works against aliens ("Wohin denn?").
  - **Aliens:** take 0 damage, sweep the whole party every 2nd turn, and can only be beaten by cheating: the tinkerer's `send_signal`, the `jammer` item, or ↑↑↓↓←→←→ from `data/cheats.json`, which works only in fights that list it.
  - **Party size:** fights with `"party": "all"` bring every living companion; the layout fits the player + 4.
- **Combat screen** (`scenes/combat/combat.tscn`, class `CombatScreen`):
  - Sprites, HP bars, status, the turn order, the brute's announced target (`!`), and a log.
  - Menus for keyboard, gamepad and mouse; target highlighting.
  - Death as a "moment" (pause, magenta log line, †).
  - A game-over panel: "Nochmal versuchen", "Letzten Spielstand laden" (hidden without a save) and a debug-only "Aufgeben".
  - Switching the language mid-fight rebuilds every text. Uses `ui/theme.tres` (checked with Codex's H2 theme: fits).
- **Test scene** `scenes/combat/test_combat.tscn`: choose the fight, job, companions (including "4 Testbegleiter" for the layout), items, the holy sword, the jammer, the death rule and the language.
- **Sprites:**
  - `player`: every contract animation.
  - `raider_scavenger` and `raider_brute`: idle, walk, combat_idle, attack, hurt, downed (plus jump, fall and skill fallbacks).
  - The pixel tool gained `from=…:n rotate=cw|ccw` (a lying pose from a standing frame).
- **Balance** (simulated, 60 fights each):
  - `raiders_street`: 100% for every job with the cook. Alone: doctor, craftsman and police 100%, scientist 53%, Beamter 1%.
  - `raiders_camp` with the full party: 100% for everyone (careful play), and companion deaths are rare (0–0.1 per fight).
  - Beamter + cook vs. the camp: 42% ("just barely").
  - The brute was raised to **HP 44, power 7** to get there.
- **Tests:** 56 passing, including every job × every fight × both languages through the real combat screen, and the SceneRouter path.

## Contract changes (the other agent must know)
Nothing was renamed or removed. **Additions:**
- `GameState.equip_weapon(item_id) -> bool`.
- `GameState.new_game()` now also puts the **start weapon into the inventory** (craftsman: crowbar), because the equipped weapon is always an inventory item. H3's holy sword: `$> GameState.add_item("holy_sword")` then `$> GameState.equip_weapon("holy_sword")`.
- `SceneRouter.is_debug_menu_open()` (Codex's H2 request). `is_in_combat()` and `is_changing_scene()` are now listed in AGENTS.md too.
- `CombatScreen.force_autopilot` / `force_speed`: test hooks for automated runs (AGENTS.md § Scene hooks).
- New data file `data/talk.json` (in `Data.all("talk")`). New rules: `talk_win_moral`, `well_fed_damage`. Enemies have `initiative`; skills have `cooldown`.

## Requests to the other agent (Codex)
- **H2 player:** please switch from reading `SceneRouter._debug_menu` to `SceneRouter.is_debug_menu_open()`.
- **Inventory UI:** the start weapon now shows up in the inventory (craftsman: crowbar). If you show the equipped weapon, `GameState.player.weapon_id` is the source, and `equip_weapon()` is available for an "equip" button.
- **H4 audio:** combat plays `music_combat` / `music_alien_zone`. On `EventBus.combat_finished`, the level should restart its own music (the combat screen doesn't know it).
- **H4 swarm:** the combat screen already uses `res://scenes/fx/alien_swarm.tscn` if it exists, calling `set_mood("idle"|"agitated"|"dispersed")`. Its origin should be the swarm's feet/center, and it's drawn as a child of the enemy slot at (455, 170).
- **HUD:** hide it while `SceneRouter.is_in_combat()`; the combat screen uses the whole 640×360.

## Questions for the humans (story/design)
1. **Talking shows its chance** ("Überzeugen (73 %)"). Do you like seeing the number, or should it stay hidden for more tension?
2. **The swarm kills downed companions** with its sweep, so in the alien fight a down companion is lost unless you cheat or revive them quickly. Too harsh, or exactly the "aliens are OP" feeling?
3. **The camp** is safe with the full party and careful play, but risky with fewer companions. Is that the difficulty you want, or should it be harder overall?
4. All combat texts are `[TEMP]` in `data/combat_text.json` and `data/talk.json`. Rewrite them freely, but keep the `{placeholders}`.

## Known issues / not done
- **Cook and tinkerer** are still placeholder shapes (drawn in H3).
- Weapon wear only lasts for one fight. A weapon with durability > 1 would be "repaired" between fights; that's irrelevant in the alpha (the holy sword has durability 1).
- Skills have no resource cost, only cooldowns.
- The test runner still prints the dialogue addon's harmless exit notice (see H0).
